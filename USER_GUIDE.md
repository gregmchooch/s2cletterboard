# Quick User Guide - S2C Letter Board v2.0

## Main Screen

### Spelling Words
1. **Tap Letters**: Touch any letter on the board to add it to your word
2. **Drag to Magnify**: Drag your finger to see letters magnified with a circle
3. **Letters in Spelled Box**: Each letter shows as a bubble at the bottom

### Managing Your Word
- **Delete a Letter**: 
  1. Tap the letter bubble in the spelled box (it highlights with a yellow background)
  2. Tap it again to delete
  3. Auto-unhighlights after 10 seconds if not tapped again

- **Store Word**:
  1. Tap the "Store" button (gold button on right side)
  2. Word is saved to history with today's date

## History/Settings

### Access History
- Tap "History/Settings" button at bottom of screen

### View Word History
- Words organized by date (most recent first)
- Delete individual words with the ? button

### Change Letter Color
1. In History view, tap "Settings" link (top right)
2. Select your preferred color
3. Color applies immediately to entire app
4. Color is saved and remembered on restart

### Available Colors
- Gold (default)
- Red
- Teal
- Blue
- Green
- Light Yellow
- Light Gray
- Purple

## Tips & Tricks

- **Accurate Selection**: Use the magnifying circle to see larger letters before tapping
- **Quick Deletion**: Letters stay highlighted for 10 seconds, giving you time to change your mind
- **Persistent History**: Your words are saved even if you close the app
- **Theme Customization**: Change letter colors anytime from Settings

## Button Reference

| Button | Location | Function |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Store | Bottom right of spelled box | Save current word to history |
| History/Settings | Bottom center | View/manage history and settings |
| Settings | Inside History modal (top right) | Change letter colors |
| ? (Close) | Top left of modals | Close history/settings |
| ? (Delete) | Next to history words | Delete individual word |
